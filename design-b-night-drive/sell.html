Certification tiers

Earned on the car’s real condition.

Platinum

The Collector’s Standard.

100% original factory body panels, flawless mechanicals, OEM warranty if still valid.

Gold

Smart Premium.

Original chassis; minor bolt-on parts may have been replaced; mechanically top-tier.

Silver

True Value.

Structurally safe, no major accidents, age-consistent wear; may need minor maintenance soon; priced lower.
