Which inspection do you need?

Inspection type
If something goes wrong at the dealership
  • Dealer refuses access? We call the dealer and escalate. You get a free reschedule or a 100% refund — plus a short note, “Your right to a pre-delivery inspection”, that you can show the dealer.
  • The car fails a check? You get a “Don’t sign yet” WhatsApp summary before handover, a defect list you can share with the dealer, and a free re-check after the fix.
