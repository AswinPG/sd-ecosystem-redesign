Body & paint, panel by panelBoth services

An Elcometer paint-depth gauge reads every panel in microns. Factory paint sits around 95–120 µm; readings above that point to a repaint or filler. We map it on a top-down diagram of the car, so you see exactly which door or bumper has history.
