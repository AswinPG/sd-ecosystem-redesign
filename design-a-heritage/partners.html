Who we work with

The same calibrated tools, the same checklist and the same honest report — whether it's one car or a thousand.

I.

Dealers

Certify your inventory and list it on SDCertified, where buyers already trust the seal.

  • Quality Certification at ₹9,999 per car, volume rates from 20 cars a month

II.

Banks & NBFCs

Inspection-backed used-car loans: lend against the car's real condition, not the seller's description.

  • VIN matched to RC, odometer checked against service history
  • AI fair-price band on every report for loan-to-value
  • Structured data via secure API or SFTP
  • Flood, accident and repaint flags surfaced upfront
III.

Insurers

Pre-insurance inspection, done where the car is — with photographic evidence for every panel.

  • Panel-wise paint depth in microns for pre-existing damage
  • Geo-tagged, time-stamped photos with remarks
  • Same-day reports for policy issuance and renewals
  • Remote-area batching with confirmed dates within 7 days
IV.

Corporate fleets

Periodic health checks, handover and de-fleet inspections for company and leased cars.

  • Scheduled Morning or Afternoon sessions at your premises
  • Battery, brake and tyre wear tracked over time
  • De-fleet cars certified and listed on SDCertified
  • Consolidated monthly GST invoicing
