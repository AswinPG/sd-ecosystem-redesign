Certification tiers

Three tiers, plainly defined.

Your tier is decided by the inspection, not by negotiation. Every listing shows exactly why it earned its tier.

Platinum

“The Collector's Standard.”

100% original factory body panels, flawless mechanicals, OEM or premium extended warranty eligible.

Gold

“Smart Premium.”

Original chassis; minor bolt-on parts may have been replaced; mechanically top-tier; warranty eligible.

Silver

“True Value.”

Structurally safe, no major accidents, age-consistent wear; may need minor maintenance soon; priced lower.

What if my car doesn't qualify for a tier?
You still receive the full 300+ point report — it's yours to keep and share. If the car isn't structurally safe we won't list it, and we'll explain exactly why. The ₹9,999 covers the inspection itself, so there are no refunds for a result you didn't hope for — but there are never surprises either.
Is there really no commission when it sells?
None. SDCertified charges 0% brokerage to buyers and sellers. The ₹9,999 Quality Certification fee is the only charge.
Can I see the report before the listing goes live?
Yes. You'll get the report on WhatsApp and email the same day, and the listing is published only after you approve the price and photos.
I'm a dealer with many cars. Is there a plan?
Yes — dealers get batch certification, a partner dashboard and priority listing. See partner plans.
How long does the certificate stay valid?
90 days or 3,000 km, whichever comes first. After that we offer a quick re-check so buyers always see a current report.
Who will the buyer pay?
The buyer pays you directly. Your guide helps with the RC transfer paperwork and can arrange a supervised test drive.
