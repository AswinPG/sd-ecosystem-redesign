Frequently asked

Questions we hear most

Can I be present during the inspection?

Yes, always. Many buyers join for the road test. If you can't be there — or you're abroad — you'll get a WhatsApp and push update at every milestone (assigned, arriving, started, report ready), and the full photo-backed report on WhatsApp and email.

What does the 10-minute slot hold mean?

When you pick a date and session, we hold that slot for you for 10 minutes so nobody else can take it while you pay. If the timer runs out, simply pick it again — if it's still open, it's yours.

My area isn't in the core service zone. Can you still come?

Yes. A visiting team serves areas like Wayanad. We gather requests in your area and dispatch when three are ready, or within 7 days of your request at the latest — so you'll always have a confirmed date within a week.

When do I receive the report?

Usually within 4 hours of the inspection finishing — on WhatsApp, by email and in My inspections. It includes section scores, paint-depth values per panel, OBD results, photos with remarks and the inspector's signature.

What if the seller won't let the technician inspect?

If the inspection can't be completed for reasons outside your control, you get a free rebooking or a full refund — your choice. That's exactly what happened with the Audi Q7 credit above.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. You see the base price and 18% GST before you pay. Travel charges apply only outside our service locations and are always shown upfront. SD Assure warranty is optional.
