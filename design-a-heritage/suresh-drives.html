Our story · Kozhikode, Kerala

It began with one question: what is this car really hiding?

In 2014, buying a used car in Kerala meant trusting a broker's word. Suresh Drives started in Kozhikode with a simple idea — take a trained inspector and the right tools to the car itself, and tell the buyer everything, plainly.

Ten years on, that idea became India's first professional on-site car inspection company: a handpicked team of technicians, many with years of experience in the Gulf, who inspect pre-owned cars, new cars at delivery, and cars for families buying from abroad. Every report carries the same promise — if we found it, you'll read it.

The 300+ point process

Six disciplines, one honest verdict.

Know the exact condition of the car before making a decision. Each stage is photographed and scored, then summarised by our AI report engine and signed by the inspector.

I.

Advanced car scanning

Full OBD diagnostics across every module — active and stored fault codes, mileage plausibility and VIN cross-check against the RC.

II.

Hidden accident repair detection

An Elcometer paint-depth gauge on every panel. Factory paint reads 95–120 µm; anything far above means a repaint, and we'll tell you where.

III.

Digital videoscope

An Autel videoscope looks inside the engine, behind panels and under the floor — rat bites, oil leaks, flood silt and welding marks.

IV.

Battery health

State-of-health and cranking tests, alternator output and electrical load — so a weak battery isn't your first surprise.

V.

Fluids, tyres, brakes

Oil, coolant and brake fluid condition; tread depth and DOT dates on every tyre; pad and disc wear measured, not guessed.

VI.

Road test

Idle, starting, acceleration, steering, transmission shift quality, CV joint noise and braking — driven by a technician who knows the model.
