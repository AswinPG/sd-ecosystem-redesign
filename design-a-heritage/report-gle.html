Body & paint

Paint depth, panel by panel.

Measured with an Elcometer gauge in microns (µm).

What's this?

A micron is a thousandth of a millimetre. Factory paint on this car reads 100–125 µm. A much thicker reading means the panel was repainted — usually after a scuff or a repair. It isn't bad in itself; it tells you where to look.

Factory · 100–125 µm Repainted · 165–190 µm

What are OBD and DTC?

OBD (on-board diagnostics) is the car's self-check system. When something goes wrong it stores a DTC — a diagnostic trouble code. "Active" codes describe a fault happening now; "stored" codes are history. This car has neither.
