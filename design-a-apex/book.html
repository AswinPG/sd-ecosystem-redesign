What’s included

Used Car Pre-Purchase · the six zones
  • Exterior & paint depth — panel-by-panel readings with an Elcometer gauge to find hidden accident repairs.
  • Engine room — fluids, leaks, belts, mountings, turbo, rat bites, exhaust smoke; Autel videoscope where needed.
  • OBD diagnostics — active and stored fault codes, explained in plain words.
  • Under the car — driveline, differential, underbody, exhaust, suspension, brakes.
  • Interior & electricals — gauges, audio & navigation, upholstery, trunk, battery health.
  • Road test — starting, idle, acceleration, steering, gear shifts, CV joints, brakes.
  • AI-powered report with photos, VIN check and a fair-price band — on WhatsApp and email.
New Car PDI · before you sign
  • VIN & build date — fresh stock, not old inventory.
  • Paint depth & panel gaps — any repaint before delivery shows up.
  • Tyres, pressures, fluids and under-body check.
  • Electricals & software — infotainment, lights, sensors, pending updates.
  • Keys, manuals, warranty booklet and accessories present.
  • “Don’t sign yet” summary on WhatsApp if anything needs fixing before handover.

If something goes wrong at the dealership

  • Dealer refuses access? We call the dealer and escalate. You get a free reschedule or a 100% refund — plus a short “Your right to a pre-delivery inspection” note you can show the dealer.
  • Car fails? You get a “Don’t sign yet” WhatsApp summary before handover, a defect list to share with the dealer, and a free re-check after the fix.
