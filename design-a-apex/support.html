FAQs

Straight answers

How long does an inspection take?

A used-car inspection takes about 3–4 hours. A New Car PDI takes about 2 hours, and we plan it to finish at least an hour before you sign.

How is the price decided?

Used cars follow the model’s segment: Standard ₹3,999, Premium ₹4,999, Luxury ₹5,999 — plus 18% GST, shown before you pay. A New Car PDI is ₹3,999 + GST = ₹4,719 for every car. Example: a 2019 Hyundai Creta is Standard · ₹4,719 incl. GST.

Are there travel charges?

Only outside our service locations, and you see them before you pay. Remote areas are served by a visiting team: we gather requests per area and confirm your date within 7 days at the latest.

Can someone else pay? (pay by link)

Yes. Pay by link — send it to anyone (any country). While the link is open we hold your slot for 2 hours; the link expires with the hold.

I live abroad. Can my family be at the car?

Yes. Add a representative (brother, father, friend…) when you book, and choose what they receive: the arrival window, milestones and/or the report. Phone numbers from the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, the US, Canada and the UK work too, and you can see your local time next to IST.

What does the seller see?

The seller sees the arrival window and milestones — never your payment details or the report.

When do refunds arrive?

To your original payment method in 3–5 working days. Full refund if you cancel 24 hours or more before the session, 50% within 24 hours, and 100% if we can’t complete for reasons on our side. Rescheduling is free up to 12 hours before.

How do I get the report?

On WhatsApp and email the moment it’s ready, and in the app under My inspections. It includes photos, scores, what we couldn’t test, and a PDF you can download and share.

Can you follow the technician on a map?

No — tracking is milestone-based. You get one WhatsApp and one push update at each step (booked, assigned, arriving today, in progress, report ready). The findings arrive together, in the report.

Spotting flood-damaged cars

  • Smell and carpets. A damp, musty smell, new carpets in an older car, or silt under the carpet edges and in the spare-wheel well.
  • Rust where it shouldn’t be. Seat rails, seat-belt buckles, pedal brackets and the screws under the dashboard.
  • Electrical faults. Flickering clusters, windows or locks that work only sometimes, fogged lamps.
  • Stored fault codes. The OBD scan shows communication faults that often follow water damage.

Every Suresh Drives report states “no flood markers” or lists what was found.

PDI checklist for new car delivery

  • VIN and build date — fresh stock, not old inventory.
  • Paint and panel gaps — a repaint before delivery shows on the paint gauge.
  • Tyres and pressures, fluids and the underbody.
  • Electricals and software — infotainment, lights, sensors, pending updates.
  • Two keys, manuals, warranty booklet and the accessories you paid for.
