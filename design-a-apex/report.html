What’s µm / paint depth?

µm (microns) are thousandths of a millimetre. The gauge measures how thick the paint is on each panel. A reading well above the factory range usually means a repaint. Repainted doors and bumpers are cosmetic; repaints on pillars or the roof would point to structural repair.

What’s a code?

The car’s computers log faults as codes (DTCs — diagnostic trouble codes), read through the OBD port. “Active” means a fault is happening now. “Stored” means the car remembers a past fault. A single cleared, non-critical communication code is common and nothing to worry about.
