Our story

Kozhikode roots. No blind buying.

Suresh Drives started in Kozhikode in 2014 with a simple idea: go to the car, check it properly, and tell the buyer the truth — in writing, with photos.

Ten years on, our Gulf-experienced technicians carry an OBD scanner, an Elcometer paint-depth gauge and an Autel videoscope to every inspection, and every report is AI-powered and photo-backed. Expert-checked cars. Zero blind buying.

Found a flaw, or couldn’t test something? You’ll see it in the report — not hear about it later.
