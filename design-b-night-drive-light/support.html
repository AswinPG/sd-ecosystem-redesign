Frequently asked

Can I see the inspection while it’s happening?

We update you at each milestone — assigned, arriving today, started, report ready — on WhatsApp and push. Findings are revealed together in the report, photo-backed and signed, so nothing is taken out of context.

How long does an inspection take?

About 3–4 hours on site for a used car, ~2 hours for a New Car PDI. The report usually follows within an hour.

What if the seller isn’t there?

We try both numbers and wait. If we still can’t inspect, you can rebook for free or get a full refund.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Free reschedule up to 12 hours before. Full refund if you cancel 24 hours or more before; 50% within 24 hours; 100% if we can’t complete for reasons on our side.

I live abroad. Can I book for family in Kerala?

Yes — choose “Pay later via WhatsApp link” at checkout and share the tracking link with your family. Reports arrive on WhatsApp wherever you are.

Are there travel charges?

Not inside our service areas. Outside, a visiting team batches nearby requests — you’ll always see any charge before paying.

If something goes wrong

  • Seller not at the car? We try both numbers and wait. Rebooking is free, or take a full refund.
  • Dealer refuses PDI access? We call the dealer and escalate. Free reschedule or 100% refund, plus a note on “Your right to a pre-delivery inspection” to show the dealer.
  • New car fails a check? A “Don’t sign yet” WhatsApp summary before handover, a defect list for the dealer, and a free re-check after the fix.
