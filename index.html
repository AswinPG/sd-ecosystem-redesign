What changed, and why

The UX principles every concept was built and validated against.

Someone is guiding me

  • Every step says where you are, what happens next and who is helping.
  • A human guide and call/WhatsApp are one tap away on every screen.
  • Tracking follows clear milestones, each sent on WhatsApp and push: Booked → Assigned → Arriving today → In progress → Report ready. The findings are revealed when the inspection is complete, so no real-time syncing is needed.

I'm in control

  • Edit any detail before paying; reschedule, cancel, change contact or share tracking yourself.
  • The 10-minute slot hold is visible, so nobody loses a slot by surprise.
  • Choose how you hear from us: WhatsApp, push, SMS fallback or email, for each event.

I feel safe

  • Prices are shown up front with GST included. There are no hidden charges.
  • The Suresh Drives seal appears wherever you make a decision. Reports show honest flaws.
  • The refund policy is written in plain words, and remote areas get a clear promised date.

Brand-led growth

  • The Suresh Drives heritage funnels customers into the SDCertified app, the only place to book.
  • Each finished report offers next steps: list this car, add SD Assure, or buy certified.
  • A partner programme for dealers, banks, NBFCs and insurers supports the scale-up.
