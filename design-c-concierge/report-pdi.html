What's µm / paint depth?

Micrometres (µm) are thousandths of a millimetre. A paint gauge measures paint thickness on each panel. Even readings across the car mean no panel was repainted — for example after transit damage.

Why does the VIN build date matter?

The VIN encodes when the car was built. A recent date means the car hasn't been sitting in a yard for months — batteries, tyres and seals are fresher.
