If something goes wrong at the showroom

The dealer refuses access. We call the dealer and escalate on your behalf. You get a free reschedule or a 100% refund, plus a short note — "Your right to a pre-delivery inspection" — you can show the dealer.

The car fails. You get a "Don't sign yet" WhatsApp summary before handover, a defect list you can share with the dealer, and a free re-check after the fix.
