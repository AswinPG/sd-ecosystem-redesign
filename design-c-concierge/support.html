Common questions

Why don't I see findings during the inspection?

Our technicians work through every check without interruptions, then review the findings before sharing them. You get milestone updates (assigned, arriving, started), and the complete report — with photos — the moment it's ready.

New Car PDI: what if something goes wrong?

The dealer refuses access: we call the dealer and escalate. You get a free reschedule or a 100% refund, plus a short "Your right to a pre-delivery inspection" note to show the dealer.

The car fails: you get a "Don't sign yet" WhatsApp summary before handover, a defect list to share with the dealer, and a free re-check after the fix.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free reschedule up to 12 hours before. Full refund if you cancel 24 hours or more before; 50% within 24 hours; 100% if we can't complete it for reasons on our side.

What if the seller isn't there?

We mark it "couldn't complete" with the reason, and you rebook free. We also remind the seller the day before to reduce no-shows.

Do you inspect outside Kozhikode?

Yes. Areas outside our core zone are served by a visiting team: we gather requests in the area (3 requests, or 7 days at most) and confirm your date. Travel charges may apply outside service locations.

Can family abroad pay for me?

Yes. Pay by link — send it to anyone (any country). While the link is open we hold your slot for 2 hours; the link expires with the hold.
