What's µm / paint depth?

Micrometres (µm) are thousandths of a millimetre. A paint gauge measures paint thickness; noticeably thicker readings than the factory panels mean that panel was repainted.

What's OBD / a DTC?

OBD is the car's self-diagnosis system. A scanner reads Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs) — error messages its computers record. "Active" means happening now; "stored" means it happened before.
