What's µm / paint depth?

Micrometres (µm) are thousandths of a millimetre. A paint gauge measures how thick the paint is. Factory paint is usually 100–125 µm; a noticeably thicker reading means that panel was repainted — often after a scrape or minor repair.

What's OBD / a DTC?

OBD is the car's own self-diagnosis system. We plug a scanner into it and read Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs) — error messages the car's computers record when something misbehaves. "Active" means happening now; "stored" means it happened in the past.
